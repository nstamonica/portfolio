# Design System: Nathan Sta Monica

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Living Home Screen"**

The portfolio reads like an Apple home screen: opaque white widgets, softly lifted, laid out as a bento on top of a slowly moving wallpaper. The wallpaper is a full-page WebGL liquid in navy, cobalt, violet and orchid folds with iridescent sky-to-peach rims, lit by a lens that follows the pointer through the gutters. Everything the visitor reads sits on a card; the liquid is atmosphere, never a reading surface, except where the page closes on a darkened band of it.

Density is high but calm. On desktop windows of 1280px and wider the whole story fits in one viewport: identity, about, a full-height experience timeline, tech, a projects teaser and education. The cards carry SF Pro (Inter off Apple platforms) in tight negative tracking, ink on white, with Apple blue reserved for actions. Colour beyond that comes from five per-role tones that tint chips, org lines, bullets and timeline nodes, so each employer has its own quiet signature.

Motion is a single authored moment, the widgets rising onto the liquid in reading order, plus two continuous interactions: the liquid lens and the glass-icon tilt on project art. Numbers stay understated: they sit in body copy at body weight, sometimes emphasised in semibold, never blown up into hero metrics.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Opaque white widget cards (28px radius) on a full-bleed WebGL liquid ground.
- Apple system type stack, weight 600 headings with tight negative tracking.
- One action blue; five per-role tones for everything categorical.
- One night card and one tone-lit art card break the white rhythm per screen.
- One-screen bento on desktop, with three height tiers that keep it on one screen.
- One staggered rise on load; everything respects reduced motion.

## Colors

A neutral Apple ink-and-white palette with one action blue, five categorical tones, and a deep liquid ground behind everything.

### Primary
- **Apple Blue** (apple-blue): the only action colour. Primary buttons (Résumé), the selected row in the command palette, focus rings and text selection tint. Hover lifts it to **Apple Blue Bright** (apple-blue-hover).
- **Link Blue** (link-blue): blue as information rather than action. The Carleton headline under the name. Never a button fill.

### Secondary (per-role tones)
Each tone is a set scoped by a `data-tone` attribute and resolved by the nearest ancestor: an ink for text, a tint for chip fills, a node colour for bullets and timeline dots, a gradient pair for fact icons, and five art stops for case art.
- **Crimson** (tone-crimson-ink on tone-crimson-tint): RCMP and the security tools chips.
- **Teal** (tone-teal-ink on tone-teal-tint): Shared Services Canada.
- **Blue** (tone-blue-ink on tone-blue-tint): Correctional Service Canada.
- **Amber** (tone-amber-ink on tone-amber-tint): Canada Revenue Agency.
- **Violet** (tone-violet-ink on tone-violet-tint): projects, including the projects teaser art.

### Neutral
- **Ink** (ink): headings, names, emphasised phrases, the dark button. **Ink Hover** (ink-hover) is the dark button's hover fill only.
- **Ink Secondary** (ink-secondary): card prose, bullets, default chip text.
- **Muted** (muted, 6.4:1 on white): supporting lines such as "previously" and section subtitles.
- **Subtle** (subtle, 5.1:1 on white, 4.6:1 on mist): counts ("4 roles"), palette hints and placeholders. The lightest grey allowed for text.
- **Line** (line) and **Hairline** (hairline): the outline-button border, timeline rail, and list dividers in the Projects section.
- **Mist** (mist): default chip fill and the search pill.
- **Night Card** (night-card): the single dark widget (Education).
- **Liquid Ground** (liquid-ground): the liquid's darkest tone. Page background, overscroll, WebGL fallback base and mobile browser chrome.
- **Liquid Fallback stops** (liquid-fallback-cobalt, liquid-fallback-violet, liquid-fallback-magenta, liquid-fallback-sky): the four radial glows painted over Liquid Ground when WebGL is unavailable or still booting. They are material, never UI colours.
- **Available Green** (available-green on available-fill, 5.8:1): the availability status pill only, with a #34c759 dot.

### Named Rules
**The One Action Blue Rule.** Apple Blue marks things you can press or that have focus. Categorical colour comes from the tones, never from the action blue.

**The Tone Scope Rule.** A tone is applied by setting `data-tone` on a container; chips, bullets, timeline nodes and org lines inside inherit it. Never hand-pick a tone hex inside a component.

**The Subtle Floor Rule.** No text lighter than Subtle (#6e6e73) on white or mist. On dark grounds, white text stays at 60% opacity or more.

## Typography

**Display Font:** -apple-system / SF Pro (with Inter via next/font, then Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial)
**Body Font:** the same stack
**Label/Mono Font:** none; key hints use the sans stack in a `kbd`

**Character:** One family doing everything, as Apple does: semibold headings tracked tight, regular body tracked slightly negative, figures tabular where they align in dates and periods.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (display, 600, 3rem, line-height 1.02, -0.036em): the name in the identity card, one per page. Tall desktop and the top of the clamp(2.375rem, 4.2vw, 3rem) used below desktop. Steps to **Display Compact** (display-compact, 2.5rem) on the compact tier and **Display Short** (display-short, 2.125rem) on the short tier.
- **Headline** (600, clamp(2rem, 4.2vw, 3.25rem), 1.06, -0.032em): the contact close. The Projects section title uses the same voice at clamp(2rem, 3.4vw, 2.625rem); project names at clamp(1.5rem, 2.6vw, 2rem), -0.028em.
- **Title Large** (title-lg, 600, 26px, -0.026em): the projects teaser title; **Title Large Short** (title-lg-short, 24px) on the short tier.
- **Title Medium** (title-md, 600, 22px, -0.022em): the education school name; it drops to Title size (19px) on the short tier.
- **Title** (600, 19px, 1.25, -0.02em): every white widget's heading ("About me", "Experience", "Tech").
- **Lede** (400, clamp(17px, 1.5vw, 20px), 1.52): the contact paragraph, max 36rem.
- **Body** (400, 17px, 1.47, -0.012em): page base and project summaries, max 60ch.
- **Body Lead** (body-lead, 500, 16px, -0.014em): the Link Blue headline line under the name.
- **Body Card** (400, 15px, 1.55): card prose, max 64ch. Experience bullets drop to 13.5px / 1.5; role names are 15.5px semibold.
- **Body Secondary** (body-secondary, 400, 14px): secondary and meta text. The "previously" line, the education degree, the teaser tagline, the contact email line, the project meta line, About copy and fact rows on the short tier, and the skip link. Compact in-card buttons (More on GitHub, project links) also set their label at 14px.
- **Label** (500, 13px): org and period lines (in the tone ink), counts, status pill, footer.
- **Chip** (500, 12px, -0.005em): tags.
- **Key Cap** (key-cap, 400, 11px): text inside `kbd` key caps only (esc, ⌘K / Ctrl K in the footer hint). Never used for running text.

### Named Rules
**The Quiet Numbers Rule.** Metrics live inside sentences at body size; emphasis is semibold ink via `**marked**` phrases in content, never a larger size, a colour, or a stat block.

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Headings and labels are sentence case at normal or negative tracking. No uppercase tracked labels above headings.

## Layout

A single centred container (1360px max, gutter clamp(16px, 3vw, 32px)) holds a CSS grid bento with named areas, followed by a full-width Projects card and the contact band.

- **Phone (<768px):** one column, 16px gaps, 16px vertical page padding: identity, about, experience, tech, education. The projects teaser is hidden because the Projects section follows directly.
- **Tablet (768–1279px):** two columns; identity, about, experience and tech span both; projects teaser and education share the last row. Page padding 32px.
- **Desktop (≥1280px):** one screen, vertically centred in 100svh. Five tracks (86fr 12fr 51fr 51fr minmax(330px, 74fr)) give row 1 identity | about and row 2 tech | projects | education, with experience spanning both rows in the right column. Experience uses `contain: size` so its list scrolls inside the card under a 40px fade mask instead of stretching the rows.
- **Height tiers on desktop:** *tall* (≥900px high) rows min 380/340px, 7-column logo grid, 28px card padding; *compact* (<900px) rows 330/280px, 10-column logo grid, 24px padding, 20px page padding; *short* (≤740px high, or ≤1365px wide and <800px high) rows 300/220px, 14-column logo grid, 12px gaps, 18px 20px padding, 12px page padding. Type steps down a notch in short.

**The One Screen Rule.** At 1280px and wider, every bento card is visible without scrolling at every height tier. New content goes in the Projects section or scrolls inside a card; it never grows the bento past the viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is real but soft: opaque cards cast a short contact shadow plus a long, negatively spread drop that reads as the widget hovering above the liquid. There is no tonal surface ladder; white is the only light surface, and the liquid is the only thing beneath it.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Widget lift** (`0 1px 2px rgb(2 4 20 / 0.08), 0 24px 48px -24px rgb(2 4 20 / 0.55)`): every white card.
- **Night lift** (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / 0.1), 0 24px 48px -24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7)`): the night card; the inset ring defines its edge against the dark liquid.
- **Glass icon** (`inset 0 1.5px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 0.8), inset 0 -16px 40px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.12), 0 40px 70px -26px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7)`): the tilted glass tile in case art only.
- **Palette float** (`0 40px 100px -20px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`): the command palette over a 30% black, 3px blur scrim.

### Named Rules
**The Opaque Widget Rule.** Reading surfaces are fully opaque. Glass (backdrop blur) is reserved for things over the liquid or art: the copy-email button, the case-art icon, the palette sheet.

## Shapes

Continuous, generous rounding throughout. Cards are 28px from 768px up and 24px on phones; the 21:9 project banner on tablets opens to 32px. Inside cards, radii shrink with size: 22px palette sheet, 13px education icon tile, 12px palette rows, 8px fact icons, 7px chips. Every button and pill is fully round (999px). The case-art glass icon uses a squircle-like 24% radius. Circles are reserved for markers: timeline nodes (11px with a white 3px ring and a tone-tint halo), 5px tone bullets, the 8px status dot.

## Components

### Buttons
Apple-style pills: quiet, fixed-height, pressed with a slight shrink.
- **Shape:** fully round (999px), 44px high by default; 40px in cards; 22px inline padding (16–18px in cards).
- **Primary:** Apple Blue fill, white text, 15px weight 500. One per group (Résumé).
- **Dark:** Ink fill, white text (Email, project links); hover to Ink Hover.
- **Outline:** white with a Line border, ink text; hover darkens the border to ink. Icon-only variants are 40px squares (36px compact) with a labelled `aria-label`.
- **Light / Glass:** for dark grounds. Light is white on the contact band; Glass is 10% white with a 24% white border and a 16px backdrop blur.
- **Hover / Focus:** 0.25s Apple ease on colour, border and transform; `:active` scales to 0.97. Focus is a 2px Apple Blue outline at 3px offset.

### Chips
- **Style:** 24px high, 7px radius, 12px weight 500, filled with the nearest tone's tint and ink (mist and ink-secondary when no tone is set). No borders.
- **State:** static tags; no selected or interactive states.

### Status Pill
Availability only: a round pill in Available Green on its pale fill, with an 8px green dot and a soft 3px ring.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 28px (24px on phones).
- **Background:** white; one night card per bento.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Widget lift or Night lift (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** none on white cards; a 1px inset white ring on the night card.
- **Internal Padding:** 28px, 24px on phones and compact desktop, 18px 20px in short.
- Headings use Title; when a card holds a scroller, the card carries the focus ring because the scroller's fade mask would clip it.

### Fact Icon
iOS Settings–style 28px tiles with an 8px radius, a vertical gradient of the tone's two node colours, a 0.5px inner top highlight, and a 17px white stroke icon at 2px stroke. Paired with 14.5px ink text in a two-column list.

### Experience Timeline
A vertical list with a 1px Line rail, 11px tone-coloured nodes ringed in white and haloed in the tone tint, a 15.5px semibold role, a 13px org · period line in the tone ink, tone bullets, and tone chips. The first role (RCMP, crimson) leads.

### Case Art (signature)
A tone-lit studio scene: a 172° gradient through the tone's three art stops, a radial floor light, a fine sheen, a glowing orb, and a frosted glass icon tile tilted in perspective (16°, -20°, -7°) that tracks the pointer and lifts 12px on hover over 0.9s expo-out. Square in the Projects section on desktop, 21:9 on tablets, 4:3 on phones; as the bento teaser it fills the card with the icon low and right, clear of the text, and the whole card is one stretched link.

### Navigation (command palette)
There is no nav bar. A search pill (36px, mist, ⌘K or Ctrl K hint) in the identity card opens a 640px palette: frosted white sheet at 90% with a 22px radius, a 56px search field at 17px, sentence-case semibold group headings in Subtle with no tracking, rows with a 36px icon tile, a 15px label and 13px detail. The selected row fills with Apple Blue and white text. The palette needs JavaScript and is hidden without it.

### Icons
One stroke family: 24px grid, 1.6px stroke, round caps and joins, `currentColor`. Sized 16–22px inline. Brand marks (GitHub, LinkedIn) and the tech logos are the only filled artwork.

### Liquid Ground (signature)
A fixed, full-page WebGL fragment shader behind every page: abyss and navy lows, cobalt highs, violet and orchid folds, a sky-orchid-peach iridescent rim, and a vignette. A lens follows the pointer. Without WebGL (and behind the canvas while it boots), four radial glows on Liquid Ground stand in: the Liquid Fallback cobalt, violet, magenta and sky stops.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every block of reading text on an opaque white or night card; the liquid shows only in gutters and the contact band.
- **Do** keep Apple Blue (#0071e3) for actions, focus and selection only.
- **Do** colour categorical things by setting `data-tone` (crimson, teal, blue, amber, violet) on the container.
- **Do** use sentence-case Title (19px, 600, -0.02em) for card headings.
- **Do** keep numbers inside sentences, emphasised at most with semibold ink.
- **Do** use the one stroke icon family (24px grid, 1.6 stroke, round caps).
- **Do** keep the desktop bento on one screen at all three height tiers.
- **Do** gate every animation and the smooth scroll behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
- **Do** meet WCAG AA: text no lighter than Subtle on light grounds and no lower than 60% white on dark.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add uppercase tracked eyebrow or kicker labels above headings.
- **Don't** use gradient-filled text; gradients belong to the liquid, case art, fact icons and the N mark.
- **Don't** use emoji as icons.
- **Don't** turn metrics into large stat blocks, counters or hero numbers.
- **Don't** make reading cards translucent; glass is for controls and art over the liquid.
- **Don't** add a second night card or a second action colour to a screen.
- **Don't** introduce a second icon style or a filled icon set.
